THE BRIEFING

Trust your team.
Or maybe don’t.

  1. Meet in a room. 4–10 people join on their own phones. Speak in person.
  2. Peek at your word. Everyone has the same word, except one player with a similar word. Nobody knows their role.
  3. Drop a clue. Take turns saying one short hint out loud. Don’t say your word!
  4. Talk. Then vote. Discuss who sounds different. Vote privately; no self-votes.
  5. Catch the Undercover. Civilians win by finding them. Undercover wins by surviving to the final 3.

A tie triggers one 30-second revote among tied players. A second tie or no votes means nobody leaves. Timed-out turns move on automatically. Eliminated players watch until the next game.
